The Environmental Footprint of AI

What the AI industry draws in as inputs, and what it sends back out as impacts. Arrows point inward for resources consumed and outward for effects produced — a system, not a sequence.

How to read this
Ongoing input — consumed every hour the system runs (power, water).
One-time input — spent once to build the system (mining, fab, construction).
One person's queries — drawn thin on purpose: minor against the build-out.
Impact — sent back out into the environment (carbon, heat, e-waste).
Cycle — hardware ages out as e-waste and is replaced, so the build-out inputs repeat. The system loops; it does not end.
