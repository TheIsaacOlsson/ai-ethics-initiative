Usage notes

  • Newsreader for all headings, quotes, and active/selected labels; Hanken Grotesk for body copy, buttons, and eyebrows.
  • Eyebrows: 13px Hanken Grotesk, uppercase, 0.14em tracking, muted color.
  • Blue (#13499B) is the default interactive accent (links, hovers, active rail). Each principle screen swaps in gold (#C9A227) as its accent instead — never mix both on one screen.
  • Radii: 8–10px for cards/buttons, full pill (999px) for rounded buttons, 50% for dots/icons.
  • Dark overlay panels sit at rgba(18,15,11, 0.45–0.7) with a 6–8px backdrop blur over imagery.
